Experience Overview

01

Garden & Homemade Tea

≈2h

Welcome to our friendly paradise where we grow premium tea, coffee, fruits and spices. Explore the garden, learn sustainable farming and try hands-on tea-making — black, green & white — from leaf to cup.

02

Factory Experience

≈2h

Discover large-scale processing: leaf withering → rolling/crushing → oxidation/fermentation → drying/firing → grading & packing. See equipment lines, experience each stage, and compare results during guided tasting.
